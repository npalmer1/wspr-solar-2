WSPR Solar 2 - what else this PC needs
======================================

WSPR Solar 2 collects the solar and geomagnetic conditions from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction
Center (A and K indices, solar flux, sunspot number, X-ray background, proton flux, flares and solar
radio bursts), shows what they mean for HF propagation, and keeps a day-by-day record of them for
WSPR Scheduler.

It needs:

1. MySQL or MariaDB with the WSPR Scheduler databases - the same ones WSPR Scheduler uses. WSPR Solar 2
   keeps its record in wspr_sol.weather. If WSPR Scheduler already runs on this PC, nothing else is
   needed. Otherwise see "The WSPR Scheduler Database Configuration" at
   https://sourceforge.net/projects/wspr-scheduler/files/
   The database password is read from DBcredential in your user folder (written by WSPR Scheduler);
   without one the default password is used.

2. An internet connection, to services.swpc.noaa.gov (and hamqsl.com for the Forecast picture).
